## 💡 Essential Tip Boxes

### 🚪 Wrong Turnstile Emergency Pass
If you accidentally tap into the wrong direction turnstile at an older station without cross-platform overpasses, tap out and tap into the correct side within 5 minutes. The system will NOT charge a double fare.

### 🚇 Line 9 Express vs. All-Stop Traps
Line 9 (connecting Gimpo Airport to Gangnam) runs both 'Express' (급행, red LED header) and 'All-Stop' (일반) trains on the same tracks. Express trains skip minor stations; check the station map before boarding.

## 📖 Guide Breakdown

### 1. The Platform Transfer Door Numbers: The 8-Minute Hack

Seoul subway station platforms have two-digit coordinates painted in bold on the platform screen doors (such as '3-2' or '7-4'). The first digit is the train car number; the second digit is the door number.

When you look up any transit route in Naver Map or KakaoMetro, look for the 'Fast Transfer' badge (빠른 환승). Boarding at the designated door means that when the train stops at your transfer station, your door opens directly aligned with the transfer staircase or escalator. In massive multi-layer interchange stations like Express Bus Terminal or Dongdaemun History & Culture Park, this saves you up to 500 meters of brisk underground hiking.

### 2. The 30-Minute Free Transfer Window

Seoul operates an integrated metropolitan transfer discount system. When you transfer between different subway lines, between subway and city buses, or between different bus routes, your subsequent journey is 100% free (or charges only a small incremental distance fee of 100–200 KRW for trips exceeding 10 km).

To qualify, you MUST tap your T-money card against the reader when stepping off your first vehicle, and tap onto your connecting vehicle within 30 minutes (extended to 60 minutes between 21:00 and 07:00).

### 3. Subway Etiquette: The Pink Seat & Silence Rules

• The Pregnant Women Seats (임산부 배려석): Marked by bright pink floor decals and pink plush seat covers in the middle of each car. Even when the train is packed and the pink seat is empty, Koreans leave it vacant out of social respect. Never sit here.

• Elderly/Disabled End Sections (노약자석): The three-seat benches at the far ends of every train car are strictly reserved for seniors (65+), pregnant women, and disabled riders. Sitting there as a young able-bodied tourist will frequently draw vocal reprimands from elderly passengers.

• Phone Calls & Music: Speaking loudly on phone calls or listening to audio without headphones is considered severe anti-social behavior on Seoul metro cars. Keep your smartphone on vibrate mode (매너모드).
