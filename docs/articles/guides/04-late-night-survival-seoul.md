## 💡 Essential Tip Boxes

### ♨️ Emergency Overnight Jimjilbang
If stranded without a room past 02:00, seek a traditional 24-hour Jimjilbang (Korean bathhouse/spa like Spa Lei or The Spa in Garden 5). An overnight entry ticket (15,000–22,000 KRW) provides hot thermal baths, pajamas, and quiet heated floor sleeping halls.

### 🚖 Midnight Taxi Surcharge
Taxi meters add a 20% to 40% midnight surcharge between 23:00 and 04:00. Use Kakao T with 'Pay to Driver Directly' to summon regular metered cabs.

## 📖 Guide Breakdown

### 1. The 00:30 Metro Curfew

Seoul subway lines begin closing their final trains between 23:45 and 00:30 (slightly later on weeknights, earlier on Sundays). Once the last train passes, the underground entrances are gated shut. Plan your departure before midnight or switch to the midnight transport grid.

### 2. The 'N-Bus' Midnight Bus Grid

Seoul operates 14 dedicated midnight bus routes (designated with an 'N', e.g., N13, N15, N26, N37, N61) running between 23:30 and 06:00. The fare is 2,500 KRW. Routes connect major nightlife districts (Hongdae, Itaewon, Gangnam, Jongno, Dongdaemun). Use Naver Map to track real-time bus locations.

### 3. 24-Hour Dining: Gukbap & Pojangmacha

Look for signs that say '24시' (24 Hours). The gold standard late-night meal is hot Suyuk Gukbap (pork soup) or Haejangguk (hangover stew). In Jongno and Euljiro, traditional soup cauldrons run continuous fire 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
