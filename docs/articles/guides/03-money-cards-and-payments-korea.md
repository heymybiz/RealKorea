## 📖 Guide Breakdown

### 1. Foreign Credit Card Acceptance (98% Success Rate)

South Korea is one of the most card-saturated economies on Earth. Contactless chip and swipe Visa and Mastercard are accepted at virtually 98% of all commercial establishments—including convenience stores, supermarkets, department stores, cafes, restaurants, and taxis.

American Express is widely accepted at major luxury hotels, duty-free stores, and department stores, but experiences frequent terminal rejections at independent neighborhood cafes and small mom-and-pop restaurants. Discover and Diners Club have very low domestic terminal penetration outside major chains.

### 2. The Apple Pay & Google Pay Reality

• Apple Pay: Works in South Korea, but ONLY at merchants that have installed modern EMV NFC contactless payment terminals. This includes all major convenience store chains (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven, Emart24), Starbucks, McDonald's, Shinsegae Department Store, and Hyundai Department Store. Smaller independent boutiques and traditional restaurants still rely on older IC chip insertion slots.

• Google Pay / Samsung Pay (Foreign Cards): Google Pay is completely unsupported in South Korea due to domestic payment routing regulations. Foreign-issued cards registered in Samsung Pay will generally fail at domestic terminals. Always carry a physical plastic credit card in your pocket.

### 3. Instant Duty-Free Tax Refunds at Register

For any single purchase between 15,000 KRW and 1,000,000 KRW at registered Tax-Free merchants (such as Olive Young, ABC Mart, Uniqlo, Gentle Monster, and flagship fashion boutiques), present your physical passport at checkout. The 7–8% VAT is deducted immediately on your receipt, eliminating the need to wait in airport customs lines.
