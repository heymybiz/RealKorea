# Confirmed Tourist Traps & Verified Local Alternatives

## ⚠️ Trap #1: Myeongdong Main Street Carts: Quadruple Prices for Frozen Tourist Snacks (Myeongdong Night Food Carts / 명동 노점 길거리 음식)

- **Location**: Line 4, Myeongdong Station, Exit 6 (Main pedestrian drag)
- **Trap Warning**: Priced 300–400% higher than traditional markets. Most items are thawed industrial pre-packs cooked on propane griddles.
- **✅ Verified Local Alternative**: Walk 15 minutes north to Gwangjang Market or take Line 4 two stops to Dongdaemun Night Market for authentic, locally-priced street food.

> **⚠️ Financial Warning**: A single grilled lobster tail here costs 18,000 KRW. For the same money, you can eat a full hot pork belly lunch with unlimited side dishes in neighboring Euljiro.

Myeongdong's central street carts emerged as an unregulated tourist spectacle. While the neon signs and grilling sizzle look tempting on social media, the food is expensive, lacks local culinary tradition, and demands cash-only transactions without receipts.

## ⚠️ Trap #2: Namdaemun Gate 1 Tout Stalls: Pre-cooked Braised Hairtail Reheats (Namdaemun Main Gate Seafood Touts / 남대문 1번 게이트 호객 식당)

- **Location**: Line 4, Hoehyeon Station, Exit 5 (Front plaza near gate)
- **Trap Warning**: Touts offer free beer or side dishes verbally, then add them to the bill or serve inferior frozen hairtail chunks.
- **✅ Verified Local Alternative**: Walk 80 meters deeper into the market alley to the authentic 'Galchi Jorim Alley' (갈치조림골목) where Hui-rak (희락) and Wangseong (왕성) braise fresh hairtail in aged brass pots with complimentary fried fish heads.

> **⚡ The Deep Alley Rule**: Never eat at the market perimeter where staff speak four languages and grab your arm. The genuine 50-year-old culinary legends are buried inside the narrow 1-meter-wide interior corridors.

Namdaemun Market is a treasure of Seoul food history, but the entrance gate stalls exploit travelers who don't know the layout. Bypass the street hawkers and head straight into the interior hairtail alley for piping hot brass-pot braises.
