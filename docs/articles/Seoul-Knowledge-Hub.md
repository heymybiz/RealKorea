## 🗺️ Neighborhood Clusters (12 Nodes)

- **[[Jongno–Gwanghwamun]]** (`jongno` / 종로·광화문) — Dynastic Joseon palace gates, 60-year-old gukbap cauldrons, and quiet stone-walled alleys untouched by glass skyscrapers.
- **[[Ikseon–Bukchon]]** (`ikseon-bukchon` / 익선·북촌) — 1920s urban hanok roofs packed tight with quiet tea rooms, craft breweries, and residential hill paths where noise rules apply.
- **[[Myeongdong–Namdaemun]]** (`myeongdong` / 명동·남대문) — Cosmetics flagships and heritage kalguksu institutions, separated by bustling wholesale alleyways and currency exchange rows.
- **[[Hongdae–Yeonnam–Mangwon]]** (`hongdae-yeonnam` / 홍대·연남·망원) — Subway line youth energy, underground vinyl bars, and the tranquil Gyeongui Line Forest Park cutting through cafe quarters.
- **[[Itaewon–Hannam]]** (`itaewon-hannam` / 이태원·한남) — Steep hillside embassy enclaves, niche fragrance ateliers, vinyl record lounges, and international spice dining.
- **[[Euljiro]]** (`euljiro` / 을지로) — Operating acrylic and printing workshops by daylight; unmarked steel doors revealing third-floor natural wine bars after dusk.
- **[[Seongsu]]** (`seongsu` / 성수) — Red-brick shoemaker warehouses converted into experimental design concept stores, champion roasteries, and pop-up portals.
- **[[Gangnam–Sinsa]]** (`gangnam-sinsa` / 강남·신사) — Ginkgo-lined Garosu-gil fashion avenues, specialty dermatological centers, and late-night charcoal hanwoo beef corridors.
- **[[Jamsil]]** (`jamsil` / 잠실) — The 123-floor Lotte World Tower needle, classical Seokchon Lake cherry blossom loops, and Songridan-gil bakeries.
- **[[Suwon]]** (`suwon` / 수원) — 5.7 kilometers of intact UNESCO brick fortress battlements, vintage fried chicken alleyways, and charcoal galbi institutions.
- **[[Paju (Heyri)]]** (`paju` / 파주) — Heyri Art Village concrete galleries, massive publisher book museums, and riverside reed fields bordering the DMZ borderlands.
- **[[ICN–Songdo]]** (`icn-songdo` / 인천공항·송도) — Yellow Sea airport gateway terminals, AREX high-speed transit arteries, and Songdo Central Park futuristic smart-city canal ways.
