# 📍  Presidential Hanok Ginseng Chicken Broth Near the Palace Wall

**Venue**: Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌 삼계탕)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[jongno]]
- **Transit**: Line 3, Gyeongbokgung Station, Exit 2 (120m straight, turn left into alley)
- **Why Local**: Thick nutty broth stewed with 30 medicinal roots, roasted sunflower seeds, and glutinous rice inside a whole young spring chicken.
- **Price Band**: $$ | **Hours**: Daily 10:00–22:00

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### 🍶 Insam-ju Cup Protocol
Every bowl arrives with a small shot cup of strong ginseng liquor (인삼주). You can drink it as an aperitif or pour it directly into the boiling soup broth to deepen the medicinal aroma.

Connected Hubs: [[jongno]], [[etiquette]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Famous as former President Roh Moo-hyun's favorite dining hall, Tosokchon occupies a sprawling traditional hanok. The rich opaque broth has a nutty, silky richness that sets the benchmark for Seoul ginseng chicken stew.
