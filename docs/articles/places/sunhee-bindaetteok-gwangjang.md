# 📍  Mungbean Pancakes Stone-Ground in the Noisy Market Center

**Venue**: Sunhee's Bindaetteok (순희네 빈대떡)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[jongno]]
- **Transit**: Line 1, Jongno 5-ga Station, Exit 8 (1 min into Gwangjang Market central crossroad)
- **Why Local**: Stone millstones grinding raw soaked mungbeans on site, shallow-fried in bubbling oil to crispy perfection.
- **Price Band**: $ | **Hours**: Daily 09:00–21:00 (Takeaway counter open till 22:00)

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### 🍶 The Makgeolli Pairing
Order one Bindaetteok (5,000 KRW) and a bottle of raw Jangsu Makgeolli (4,000 KRW). Dip the hot pancake into the pickled soy onion bowl before every bite.

Connected Hubs: [[jongno]], [[etiquette]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Gwangjang Market is crowded, but Sunhee's central frying stalls remain the gold standard for bindaetteok. The outer crust achieves potato-chip crispiness while the center retains a fluffy, nutty bean texture packed with minced pork and bean sprouts.
