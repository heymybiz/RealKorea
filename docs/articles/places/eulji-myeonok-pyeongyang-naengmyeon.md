# 📍  Subtle Clear Beef Broth & Fresh Buckwheat Noodles

**Venue**: Eulji Myeonok (을지면옥)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[jongno]]
- **Transit**: Line 3, Jongno 3-ga Station, Exit 9 (4 mins towards Nakwon)
- **Why Local**: The legendary Uijeongbu-style cold buckwheat noodle dynasty. Clear chilled broth dusted with red chili powder and scallions.
- **Price Band**: $$ | **Hours**: Mon–Sat 11:30–21:00 (Break 15:00–17:00, Closed Sunday)

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### 🥢 Eating Etiquette
Never add vinegar or mustard before taking your first three sips of pure broth. The subtle beef-and-pheasant depth is what Pyongyang naengmyeon purists worship.

## 📖 Editorial Notes

First-timers often describe Pyongyang cold noodles as 'tasting like cold water.' By your third visit, the delicate savoriness of the chilled beef water and the earthy fragrance of high-percentage buckwheat noodles become intensely addictive.
