# 📍  The 40-Year Gamjatang Cauldron of Euljiro's Print District

**Venue**: Dongwon Jip (동원집)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[euljiro]]
- **Transit**: Line 3, Euljiro 3-ga Station, Exit 3 (2 mins on foot through printing alley)
- **Why Local**: Rich pork backbone broth simmered in massive stainless vats with tender whole potatoes. Zero tourist inflation.
- **Price Band**: $ | **Hours**: Mon–Sat 10:00–22:00 (Break 15:30–16:30, Closed Sunday)

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### ⚡ Lunch Queue Strategy
Office workers flood the alley at 11:45. Arrive at 11:20 or after 13:15 to sit immediately without waiting in the cold.

### ⚠️ Word-of-Mouth Caution
Desk Note: While regular portions are 10,000 KRW, locals report that bone refill soup (gukmul) is complimentary upon polite request, though policies may vary during peak rush.

Connected Hubs: [[euljiro]], [[etiquette]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Hidden among lathe workshops and paper warehouses, Dongwon Jip has fed three generations of blue-collar pressmen. The signature gamjaguk (pork spine soup) comes piled with tender vertebrae that fall apart with chopsticks. Unlike franchised spots, they do not mask the meat with heavy MSG powder.
