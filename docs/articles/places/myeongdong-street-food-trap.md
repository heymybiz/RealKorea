# ⚠️ TRAP:  Myeongdong Main Street Carts: Quadruple Prices for Frozen Tourist Snacks

**Venue**: Myeongdong Night Food Carts (명동 노점 길거리 음식)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[myeongdong]]
- **Transit**: Line 4, Myeongdong Station, Exit 6 (Main pedestrian drag)
- **Why Local**: Mass-manufactured frozen lobster tails and cheese skewers priced at 15,000–20,000 KRW each. No locals buy here.
- **Price Band**: $$$ | **Hours**: Daily 17:00–23:00

## ⚠️ Tourist Trap Analysis

> Priced 300–400% higher than traditional markets. Most items are thawed industrial pre-packs cooked on propane griddles.

### ✅ Recommended Local Alternative
Walk 15 minutes north to Gwangjang Market or take Line 4 two stops to Dongdaemun Night Market for authentic, locally-priced street food.

Connected warning hub: [[traps]], [[myeongdong]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Myeongdong's central street carts emerged as an unregulated tourist spectacle. While the neon signs and grilling sizzle look tempting on social media, the food is expensive, lacks local culinary tradition, and demands cash-only transactions without receipts.
