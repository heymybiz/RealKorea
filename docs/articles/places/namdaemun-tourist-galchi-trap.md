# ⚠️ TRAP:  Namdaemun Gate 1 Tout Stalls: Pre-cooked Braised Hairtail Reheats

**Venue**: Namdaemun Main Gate Seafood Touts (남대문 1번 게이트 호객 식당)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[myeongdong]]
- **Transit**: Line 4, Hoehyeon Station, Exit 5 (Front plaza near gate)
- **Why Local**: Aggressive street hawkers pulling foreign tourists into front stalls serving cold, reheated fish stew with hidden charges.
- **Price Band**: $$ | **Hours**: Daily 08:00–20:00

## ⚠️ Tourist Trap Analysis

> Touts offer free beer or side dishes verbally, then add them to the bill or serve inferior frozen hairtail chunks.

### ✅ Recommended Local Alternative
Walk 80 meters deeper into the market alley to the authentic 'Galchi Jorim Alley' (갈치조림골목) where Hui-rak (희락) and Wangseong (왕성) braise fresh hairtail in aged brass pots with complimentary fried fish heads.

Connected warning hub: [[traps]], [[myeongdong]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Namdaemun Market is a treasure of Seoul food history, but the entrance gate stalls exploit travelers who don't know the layout. Bypass the street hawkers and head straight into the interior hairtail alley for piping hot brass-pot braises.
