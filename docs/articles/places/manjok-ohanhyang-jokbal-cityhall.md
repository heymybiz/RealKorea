# 📍  Spiced Five-Flavor Braised Pork Trotters with Hot Garlic Dip

**Venue**: Manjok Ohyang Jokbal (만족오향족발 시청본점)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[jongno]]
- **Transit**: Line 1/2, City Hall Station, Exit 8 (2 mins behind main avenue)
- **Why Local**: Specialized warm metal hotplates built into tables keep the gelatinous braised pork skin at the perfect melt-in-mouth temperature.
- **Price Band**: $$ | **Hours**: Daily 11:30–22:00 (Sat/Sun 12:00–22:00)

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### 🥟 Complimentary Manduguk
Every jokbal order comes with a free bubbling pot of rice cake and dumpling soup (tteok-manduguk) to cleanse your palate between rich pork bites.

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Manjok revolutionized Seoul jokbal by simmering pork shanks in five Chinese spices and serving them with shredded raw cabbage soaked in a tart, chilled garlic vinegar sauce that cuts right through the pork fat.
