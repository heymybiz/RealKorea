# 📍  Micro-Brewery Taproom Inside a 1920s Restored Urban Hanok

**Venue**: Artmonster Ikseon (아트몬스터 익선동점)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[ikseon-bukchon]]
- **Transit**: Line 1/3/5, Jongno 3-ga Station, Exit 4 (Cross street directly into Ikseon-dong maze)
- **Why Local**: Award-winning Gunpo craft beers served beneath traditional wooden rafters and interior rain-courtyards.
- **Price Band**: $$ | **Hours**: Mon–Fri 17:00–23:30, Sat 15:00–23:30, Sun 15:00–22:30

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### 🍺 Recommended Pour
Try the 'First Love Hyanggi' (Belgian Witbier) or the dark 'Ssuk-maek' (Mugwort Porter) paired with their wood-fired truffle pizza.

Connected Hubs: [[ikseon-bukchon]], [[etiquette]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

While much of Ikseon-dong has shifted toward dessert trends, Artmonster preserved the architectural integrity of its 100-year-old hanok framework, creating one of downtown's best atmosphere-to-quality beer destinations.
