# 📍  Cast-Iron Hanwoo Sirloin on Beef Suet Since 1964

**Venue**: Daedo Sikdang (대도식당 왕십리본점)

- **Neighborhood Node**: [[seongsu]]
- **Transit**: Line 2, Sangwangsimni Station, Exit 2 (5 mins on foot)
- **Why Local**: No marinating, no gimmicks. Premium Korean Hanwoo sirloin seared in heavy cast-iron pans greased with rendered kidney fat.
- **Price Band**: $$$$ | **Hours**: Daily 11:00–22:00

## 💡 Table Tip Box

### 🍚 The Kkakdugi Fried Rice Finale
Never leave without ordering 'Kkakdugi Bokkeumbap' (깍두기 볶음밥). The staff dump minced fermented radish and juices into the beef fat remaining in the pan, crisping it against the cast iron.

Connected Hubs: [[seongsu]], [[etiquette]]

## 📖 Editorial Notes

Daedo Sikdang invented the cast-iron beef format in Korea. The menu has exactly one meat item: Hanwoo Deungsim (ribeye/sirloin). The intense heat of the heavy pan creates an immediate caramel crust while preserving buttery juiciness.
