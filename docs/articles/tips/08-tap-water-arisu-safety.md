## 💡 Local Insider Tip Boxes

### 🚰 Arisu Municipal Quality
Seoul's municipal water brand 'Arisu' (아리수) is rigorously purified and tested for 350 quality indicators—exceeding WHO drinking water standards. You can safely brush your teeth and boil water anywhere across the capital.

### 🏢 Pre-1994 Building Considerations
In older red-brick villas or unrenovated traditional structures built before the 1994 galvanized pipe ban, mineral sediment can accumulate in standing pipes. For daily unboiled drinking, grab a 2-liter bottle of Jeju Samdasoo at convenience stores (~1,700–1,900 KRW).

### 🍽️ Restaurant Water Dispensers
Complimentary water carafes provided at dining tables come from multi-stage commercial filtration dispensers (Coway, ChungHo, SK Magic). It is clean, chilled, and universally safe to drink.

## 📖 Article Content

### 1. The Arisu Purification Standard

The Seoul Metropolitan Government operates state-of-the-art membrane and ozone purification facilities for its tap water brand, Arisu. Regular laboratory analyses consistently show that municipal water meets international safety benchmarks.

### 2. Building Vintage & Pipe Infrastructure

In 1994, South Korea banned the use of corrosion-prone galvanized steel pipes in residential construction. Modern hotels and newly built officetels utilize stainless steel and PVC plumbing. In older residential alleys, residents typically use filtered dispensers or boil water for cooking and tea.

### 3. Practical Travel Protocol

• Teeth Brushing & Washing: Completely safe with tap water in all accommodations. • Boiling Water: Electric kettles are ideal for coffee and ramen. • Raw Drinking Water: Enjoy filtered restaurant water or pick up affordable bottled water at any convenience store.
