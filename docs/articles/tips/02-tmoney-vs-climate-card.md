# T-money vs. Climate Card: Which transit pass do you actually need?

> **Dek**: With Seoul's 1,550 KRW subway fare, the 3-day Climate Card (13,000 KRW) breaks even at 4 rides per day. Take 3 rides or fewer, and standard T-money is cheaper.

## 💡 Local Insider Tip Boxes

### ⚡ The 4-Rides-A-Day Break-Even Formula
A 3-day Tourist Climate Card costs 10,000 KRW plus a 3,000 KRW non-refundable card fee (13,000 KRW total). With Seoul's base subway fare at 1,550 KRW, you need 9 separate trips over 3 days (13,950 KRW) to save money. If you ride 3 times a day or fewer, standard T-money is more economical.

### 🚫 Boundary & Excluded Line Rules
Climate Cards cover all Seoul metro lines (Lines 1–9), plus expanded suburban zones like Gimpo Goldline, Goyang, Gwacheon, and Namyangju. However, it remains strictly EXCLUDED on the AREX Non-Stop Express and red suburban express buses.

### 💳 Foreign Credit Card Top-Up Update
New ticket vending machines in major central stations allow international credit card payments for Climate Card passes (with an approximate 3.7% international gateway fee). Traditional T-money reloads at station kiosks still require cash KRW banknotes.

## 📖 Article Content

### 1. The 1,550 KRW Base Fare Economics

Seoul subway base fares are 1,550 KRW for the first 10 kilometers with free bus transfers within 30 minutes. The Tourist Climate Card is sold in short-term passes (1-day: 5,000 KRW, 2-day: 8,000 KRW, 3-day: 10,000 KRW, 5-day: 15,000 KRW, 7-day: 20,000 KRW). Note that the older 30-day commuter pass has transitioned to specialized national passes.

To determine if a pass is right for you, calculate your daily trip count. Central Seoul is exceptionally walkable: walking between Jongno, Ikseon-dong, and Euljiro takes under 10 minutes. If your itinerary involves 1 morning ride, 1 afternoon ride, and 1 dinner ride (3 trips = 4,650 KRW per day), standard T-money is cheaper. If you plan extensive hopping across 4+ destinations daily (Hongdae → Gangnam → Seongsu → Dongdaemun), the Climate Card generates immediate savings.

### 2. The 3,000 KRW Physical Card Investment

Foreign travelers must purchase a physical plastic Climate Card for 3,000 KRW at station customer service centers or convenience stores. This 3,000 KRW fee is non-refundable. Factor this initial cost into your break-even calculation before purchasing.

### 3. When to Choose T-Money or WOWPASS Instead

If you are traveling beyond Seoul into Gyeonggi-do (e.g., Suwon Hwaseong Fortress, Everland) or combining trips with Incheon and Busan, standard T-money or an all-in-one WOWPASS card offers nationwide interoperability without regional boundary confusion.
