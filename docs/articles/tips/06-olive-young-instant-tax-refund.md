## 💡 Local Insider Tip Boxes

### 🛂 Physical Passport is Legally Mandatory
Korean tax law strictly requires cashiers to scan the machine-readable chip of your original physical passport. Digital smartphone photos, photocopies, or foreign driver's licenses are rejected by the tax terminal.

### 🧴 100ml Liquid Airport Seizure Trap
Toner pads, sunscreens, serums, and ampoules exceed the 100ml aviation carry-on limit. If you pack them in your cabin backpack, airport security scanners will confiscate them. Always pack cosmetics inside checked luggage.

### 🛍️ Skip Myeongdong Flagship Lines
The Olive Young Myeongdong Town flagship often has 30-minute checkout queues. Standard neighborhood branches (Jongno 3-ga, Euljiro, Sinsa) stock identical skincare products with zero checkout lines.

## 📖 Article Content

### 1. The 15,000 KRW Immediate Deduction Mechanism

South Korea provides an immediate on-site duty-free system (Immediate Tax Refund) for international tourists. When shopping at registered retailers like Olive Young, ABC Mart, and major fashion flagships, purchases between 15,000 KRW and 1,000,000 KRW per transaction qualify for on-the-spot deduction (up to an aggregate limit of 5,000,000 KRW per trip).

Present your physical passport to the cashier before payment. The register terminal validates your tourist entry status and deducts the value-added tax directly, resulting in an effective 7% to 8% net discount after administrative processing fees, eliminating airport customs refund lines entirely.

### 2. Packing Guidelines for Airport Security

Unlike closed airport duty-free items, items purchased with immediate tax refund in the city do not require special sealed security bags. However, remember international aviation liquid restrictions: any liquid, cream, gel, or aerosol container larger than 100ml must be stowed in checked baggage before passing security.
