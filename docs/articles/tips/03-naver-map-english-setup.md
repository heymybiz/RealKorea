## 💡 Local Insider Tip Boxes

### ⚡ The 9-to-10 Digit Phone Search Hack
Translating Korean restaurant names into romanized English often yields zero search results. Instead, copy the venue's Korean landline phone number (e.g., '02-2265-3388') from Google or Instagram and paste it directly into Naver Map's search bar. It locates the exact building pin instantly.

### 🚪 'Fast Transfer' Car & Door Numbers
Look for the small two-digit number (e.g., '3-2' or '7-4') on your subway route overview. Boarding at that exact car and door aligns you directly with the transfer escalator at your destination station.

### 🌐 Setting Naver Map to English
In Naver Map settings, set the language to English. This automatically translates subway line names, station exits, and major road intersections while keeping precise GPS navigation active.

## 📖 Article Content

### 1. The Legal Reality: Why Google Maps Lacks Walking Directions

Under the Act on the Establishment and Management of Spatial Data (공간정보의 구축 및 관리 등에 관한 법률) and related national security regulations, high-precision 1:5,000 mapping data cannot be exported to foreign servers without government clearance. Because Google processes map data on global cloud infrastructure, it cannot provide turn-by-turn pedestrian routing in South Korea.

Google Maps can display general subway transit lines, but it fails on alley navigation, bus arrival countdowns, and pedestrian shortcuts. For reliable navigation in Seoul, Naver Map and KakaoMap are indispensable.

### 2. Mastering English Search in Naver Map

Naver Map has comprehensive English UI support. To find independent cafes, vintage boutiques, or heritage soup houses without complex romanization spelling, search by the venue's Korean landline or mobile phone number, or paste the official Korean road-name address (도로명주소).
