Actionable Field Protocol for Inadvertent Boundary Rejection

If you board inside Seoul on a valid line and disembark at an excluded station (e.g. Pangyo Station on the Shinbundang line), the turnstile gate will fail. Do not force the gate. Press the red "Help (도움)" bell next to the handicap wide turnstile and explain to the agent: 기후동행카드 승차 (Climate Card Boarding). You will pay only the segment fare differential in cash without a 30x penalty.
