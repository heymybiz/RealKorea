AREX Express vs All-Stop: The Basement 7 (B7) Transfer Trap at Seoul Station

Why the 43-minute non-stop express train can actually cost you 15 minutes more than the commuter train if your final hotel is in Myeongdong or Jongno.

⚠️ THE B7 ELEVATOR BOTTLENECK (FACT AUDIT)

The AREX Express terminates at Basement Level 7 (B7). If you carry standard 28-inch luggage, you cannot easily drag it up 4 flights of escalators during rush hour. You are forced to queue for 2 tiny elevators, which often backs up for 12 to 15 minutes with overseas arrivals.
