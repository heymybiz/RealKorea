🧭 CHRONOLOGICAL FIELD COMPANION

Your First 72 Hours in Seoul, Decoded Hour by Hour.

Follow this verified chronological sequence to eliminate 100% of first-timer arrival stress, currency confusion, and transit gate errors.

1
HOUR 0 ~ 1 INCHEON AIRPORT T1 / T2

1. Airport Survival: SIM, Cash & Transit Card

Do not stand in 40-minute airport SIM lines. Activate an eSIM QR code instantly. Skip the airport bank exchange tellers (8.5% loss); buy a WOWPASS or T-money card from the station convenience store.

✓ Withdraw ₩30,000 cash for metro card
⚠️ Never exchange full budget at airport
2
HOUR 2 ~ 3 SEOUL STATION TRANSIT

2. The AREX Train Choice: Avoid the B7 Trap

If staying in Myeongdong or Jongno, choose the All-Stop Commuter Train (₩4,750) arriving at Basement 3. The non-stop Express train arrives at Basement 7 and forces a 15-minute elevator wait with heavy bags.

Destination: Line 4 (Myeongdong) All-Stop is 5m Faster Door-to-Door
3
HOUR 5 ~ 7 DINNER & FIRST NIGHT

3. Solo K-BBQ Rules & Free Side Dish Refills

Korean barbecue tables require charcoal heating and provide 8 free side dishes (banchan). If you are dining alone, you must say "2인분 주세요 (2 portions please)" to be seated comfortably.

All side dishes (kimchi, garlic, lettuce) are 100% free refills "이모님 리필해주세요!"
4
DAY 2 ~ 3 SHOPPING & LATE NIGHT

4. Olive Young Instant Tax Refund & Midnight N-Buses

Bring your physical passport to any Olive Young store: purchases over ₩15,000 get an immediate 7% cash deduction at the cashier. When subways close at midnight, tap your T-money on N-buses or head to a 24h jjimjilbang.
