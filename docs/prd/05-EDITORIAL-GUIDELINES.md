# Real Korea Editorial Guidelines & Voice Protocol (PRD 05)

## 1. Core Philosophy: The Living Seoulite Voice
Real Korea is not a generic tourism brochure or an AI aggregator. It is an authoritative, documentary-grade editorial publication created for travelers who want truth over hype.

---

## 2. The 5 Anti-AI Writing Rules (De-AI Style Guide)

### Rule 1: Zero Banned AI Clichés & Buzzwords
Never use predictable LLM filler words or corporate fluff:
* 🚫 **Banned Words**: *delve, leverage, tapestry, vibrant, culinary journey, nestled in, testament to, robust, multifaceted, nuanced, pivotal, furthermore, moreover, in conclusion, whether you are X or Y*.
* ✅ **Replacement**: Concrete sensory nouns, exact numbers, and direct actions (*loud, 40-year-old, hidden underground, walk, avoid, grab*).

### Rule 2: Burstiness & Sentence Rhythm
* Vary sentence length intentionally. Mix ultra-short 3–5 word punchy sentences with detailed multi-clause observations.
* Break predictable symmetries (never write formulaic "3 pros, 3 cons, conclusion" structures).

### Rule 3: Micro-Specificity (No Averages)
* Always specify subway exit numbers, physical floors, gate numbers, exact KRW prices, and closing hours.
* Cite specific landmarks: e.g., *"Next to Baggage Carousel 9"* instead of *"At the airport"*.

### Rule 4: Conversational Parentheticals
* Use parenthetical asides `(...)` to share streetwise observations and cultural nuance:
  * *(Tip: Korean cabbies sound aggressive when they are just in a rush).*
  * *(Korean bus drivers will close doors immediately once all 45 seats fill).*

### Rule 5: Conclusion-First Architecture
* Deliver the most critical advice or financial/transit risk in the very first sentence. Never hide the answer at the bottom of the page.

---

## 3. Strict Anti-Hallucination & Verification Protocol

### Tier 1: Verified Local Fact (Crimson Checkmark `正`)
* Requires direct verification: official transit timetable, actual receipt price, physical entrance sign, or field testing.
* Stated as clear, definitive instruction.

### Tier 2: Unverified Tip / Local Hearsay / Seasonal Shifts (`⚠️ Word of Mouth`)
* If a tip comes from community feedback, taxi drivers, or local rumors that cannot be 100% guaranteed for every traveler, it MUST carry an explicit verification caution:
  * 💬 *"Desk Caution: While locals report that X works, policies can shift without notice—always confirm at the counter before boarding/ordering."*
  * 💬 *"Community Note: Some stalls accept cash-only discounts, but official tax law requires card machines upon request."*

---

## 4. Documentary Visual Photography
* Every Tip, Place, and Guide must carry an authentic 16:9 documentary plate photograph.
* Include descriptive captions and the `正 Documentary Plate` seal.
