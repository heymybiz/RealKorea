# v1 notes — Real Korea visual direction

Status: **review**  
Date: 2026-08-30

## Intent (canonical)

- Time Out city-guide IA and card rhythm, **not** Time Out “Best 50” rankings.
- Kinfolk / Monocle whitespace and photography.
- Palette: hanji paper `#F4EFE6`, ink `#161513`, dancheong crimson `#B43A3A`, brass used rarely.
- Photography: lived-in Seoul (alleys, CU at night, markets). No palace postcards, no hanbok-for-tourists cliché as the hero.
- UI language: **English**. Hangul appears only as first-mention romanization helper, e.g. Gwangjang Market shown once with 광장시장.

## Treat as direction, not spec

These stills are image-generated. Ignore:

- Misspellings in the JPEG (`DRIINK`, `YIPS`, `KORF A`)
- Extra nav items that are not in the product nav
- Random Korean headlines in the hero
- “Write a review” as a primary CTA on Place (not in MVP)
- Browser-chrome / device-frame artifacts

## What v1 already gets right

- Paper ground + crimson as a single accent, not a rainbow brand.
- Hero is a cover story + search, not a SaaS dashboard.
- Tip cards are short and documentary.
- Place page is editorial column + fact stack + map embed.
- Ask a Local looks like a magazine desk, not Reddit.
