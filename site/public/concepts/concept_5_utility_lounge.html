Can I tap my foreign Apple Pay directly at Seoul subway gates? +

No. Seoul metro gates require an offline contactless transit chip (T-money / Cashbee / Climate Card RF protocol). Foreign Apple Pay works only at retail merchant terminals with EMV contactless certification (e.g. McDonald's, 7-Eleven, Olive Young), but NOT on subways or public buses.

Solution: Purchase a physical T-money card or WOWPASS at any airport convenience store.

Is tap water safe to drink in Seoul hotels and restaurants? +

Yes, 100% scientifically safe. Seoul tap water ('Arisu') is tested across 350 parameters and exceeds WHO drinking standards. However, older buildings may have rusty internal plumbing lines.

Local practice: Most Koreans drink filtered water from dispensers (Coway/ChungHo) provided free in restaurants and hotel lobbies.

Can one person order alone at a Korean BBQ (Samgyeopsal) restaurant? +

Yes, but you must order 2 servings minimum (2인분). K-BBQ tables require charcoal heating and provide 6~10 free side dishes (banchan), which is unprofitable for a single 1-portion order.

Tip: Say "2인분 주문할게요 (I will order 2 portions)" to be welcomed without awkwardness.

Why are there no public trash cans on Seoul streets? +

Seoul removed most street bins in 1995 to enforce Korea's strict volume-based waste fee system (Jongnyangje). Locals carry their trash or dispose of coffee cups at the take-out cafe counter or next to subway ticket gates.

Warning: Illegal street dumping carries fines up to ₩1,000,000.
